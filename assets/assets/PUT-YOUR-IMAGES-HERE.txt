Drop your images and files in this "assets" folder, then reference them from index.html.

Suggested files:
- my-photo.jpg               → your professional portrait (hero photo, portrait ratio ~4:5)
- og-image.jpg               → social share image, 1200 x 630 px
- Satyam-Rai-Resume.pdf      → your resume (Download Resume button)
- client logos               → e.g. raj-hospitals.png, gnathos-dental.png ...
- project screenshots        → e.g. visioneyecentre.jpg ...
- ranking / analytics / GBP screenshots for the Gallery & Case Studies

To use one, replace the matching grey placeholder box in index.html with:
  <img src="assets/your-file.jpg" alt="describe the image" loading="lazy" />
